ORGANIZATION CONTRACT MANAGEMENT POLICY (INTERNAL)

This internal policy describes how business owners at Northwind Analytics manage vendor contracts. It is guidance for staff only and is not a contract.

1. Renewal Reviews
1.1 Business owners should begin renewal review ninety (90) days before the expiry of a vendor contract.
1.2 Finance should be consulted before any renewal decision for vendor contracts above USD 100,000 per year.

2. Reporting
2.1 Monthly vendor reports should be filed in the contract register within five (5) business days of receipt.
